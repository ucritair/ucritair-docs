Settings reference

Everything under the gear tile, in the order it appears.

Menu map#

MENU ├─ SETTINGS │ ├─ DEVICE NAME │ ├─ NEW MESSAGE TONE checkbox │ ├─ TIME & TIMEZONE │ ├─ NAME │ ├─ AUDIO VOLUME slider │ ├─ WIFI → Mesh and Wi-Fi │ ├─ MESH → Mesh and Wi-Fi │ ├─ KEYBOARD → Typing │ ├─ USB KEYBOARD/MOUSE hardware only → Extras │ ├─ DISPLAY │ ├─ PARTY hardware only → Extras │ ├─ STORAGE hardware only → Extras │ └─ EXTRAS hardware only │ ├─ LEGACY HANDHELD GB Emulator → Extras │ └─ DOOM → Extras ├─ MY ADDRESS → Chat ├─ CRYPTO → Crypto ├─ POWER │ ├─ SLEEP │ └─ POWER DOWN └─ DEVELOPER ├─ INFO └─ CONTROLS

Changes apply immediately. When you leave Settings after changing something, CAT saves your settings to permanent storage.

DEVICE NAME#

Your CAT's name: shown in the home screen's top bar, on your ID card in Explore, and to everyone you message (it is your LXMF display name). The keyboard opens straight away; press SAVE. Up to 31 printable characters, with no spaces at either end. Leave it empty to go back to the default, cat-game.

Messages: Name saved, Use printable text, without spaces at either end., or Changed for now; could not save. Please retry.

NAME, further down the list, is an older editor for the same name. Changing either changes both.

NEW MESSAGE TONE#

Tick it to hear a soft three-note chime when a message arrives, at most once every 2 seconds. Off by default. New messages wake the screen either way.

TIME & TIMEZONE#

The window LOCAL DATE & TIME shows the current local time, date and time zone.

RowWhat it does
CHOOSE BY LOCATIONThe region, country and city picker from first boot.
USE UTCJump straight to the confirm page for Coordinated Universal Time.
EDIT DATESet year, month and day.
EDIT TIMESet hour (12-hour, with AM or PM), minutes and seconds.

In the date and time editors, ◀ ▶ choose a field, ▲ ▼ change it (hold to repeat; rolling past the end carries into the next field), A saves and B cancels. A time skipped by a daylight-saving change is refused with Skipped by DST or outside 1970-2100.. A time that happens twice when clocks go back asks THIS TIME OCCURS TWICE and lets you pick FIRST (EARLIER) or SECOND (LATER).

The time zone only affects what you see. Messages carry universal time.

AUDIO#

One slider, VOLUME, in ten steps from silent to full. Use ◀ ▶ (hold to repeat) or tap either half. It sets the volume of the chime, Party music and the GB Emulator.

Volume resets on restartIn this firmware the volume isn't saved; CAT starts at full volume each time.

DISPLAY#

The status line shows whether the backlight is on, dimmed or off, and its current level.

RowChoicesDefault
BRIGHTNESS10% to 75% in steps of 575%
DIM AFTERNEVER, 15 S, 30 S, 45 S, 1 MIN, 2 MIN, 5 MIN, 10 MIN45 S
SCREEN OFF AFTERNEVER, 30 S, 1 MIN, 2 MIN, 5 MIN, 10 MIN, 30 MIN, 60 MIN2 MIN
RESET TO DEFAULTSRestores the three values above (no confirmation)–

Dimmed is half brightness. The countdown restarts with every button press, every touch and every incoming message. The press that wakes a dark screen is ignored, so a blind tap can't do anything by accident. Screen off doesn't stop CAT: radios, sensors and mail keep running.

POWER#

SLEEP and POWER DOWN (which asks Power down? first) both save everything and switch CAT off. See turning CAT off.

If saving fails, CAT stays on and shows Save failed State remains in RAM. Keep power connected.. Plug it in and try again. If the network can't shut down cleanly you see Shutdown paused.

DEVELOPER#

Diagnostics and maintenance tools. They are safe to look at; the CONTROLS actions are not.

INFO#

A set of pages. ◀ ▶ move between them (they wrap), B leaves.

PageShows
COREPlatform and device ID.
TEXTA font test page, with a small keyboard test.
MEMORYStorage and memory use.
LOGOnly in test builds; normal builds say Log recording is disabled.
AIREvery air reading with its score out of 100, including the hidden temperature and humidity, and the overall score.
POWERBattery percentage, cell voltage, charge or drain rate with a time estimate, charger phase (not charging, pre-charge, fast (CC), taper (CV), done (full)...) and whether a charger is connected.
TIMEClock values.
COMMSWi-Fi, upload and crypto job states.
FLAGSExplore story flags. ▲ ▼ select, A toggles. Changing these can break your story.
COUNTERSExplore counters (such as health). A adds one, SELECT takes one away.

CONTROLS#

No confirmationThese act the moment you press A.
RowWhat it does
ENTER BOOTLOADERSaves, then restarts the game processor in USB update mode for the provisioner. Only use it when you are about to update the firmware.
FORCE SAVESaves settings, game progress and the messaging node now. There is no message on screen.
CLEAR GAME SAVEErases your Explore progress and restarts CAT. Messages and settings are kept.
CLEAR SETTINGSErases the basic settings file and restarts CAT.

There is no firmware version screen. Connect to the USB shell and type version to see it (see the USB shell).
