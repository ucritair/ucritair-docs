Getting started

The hardware, turning CAT on and off, the first-boot time zone prompt, the home screen, and the one habit that keeps your data safe.

The hardware#

CAT has a 320×240 colour touch screen, eight buttons, a USB-C port, a microSD slot, a speaker, a set of air-quality sensors, a LoRa radio and Wi-Fi. A second, black-and-white e-paper screen shows the latest air readings even while the device is off.

What's inside: four processors

You never have to think about this, but it explains some behaviour (for example why Wi-Fi settings survive a reset of the game).

  • Game processor (RP2350). Runs the screen, the apps, the Explore game and the mesh node that sends and stores messages.
  • Board controller (Nordic nRF5340 with nRF7002 Wi-Fi). Power, charging, battery gauge, Wi-Fi and saved networks, sensors, the e-paper screen, the clock and the NFC tag.
  • LoRa radio (RAK11300). Runs RNode radio firmware.
  • Crypto chip (Baochip). Does the homomorphic encryption used by the Crypto menu.

Power and charging#

Turning CAT on#

Plug in USB-C, or press the power button. A CAT that was put to sleep, powered down or ran flat wakes this way. The e-paper screen changes from ASLEEP to AWAKE Waiting for sensors..., and the first air readings appear about 30 seconds later.

Turning CAT off#

Open Settings → POWER and choose SLEEP, or POWER DOWN and then Yes. Both do the same thing in this firmware. CAT shows Saving..., saves everything, shows GOODNIGHT and switches its battery off. The e-paper screen then shows a sleeping unicorn with Powered off. and Plug in USB-C to turn on.

Screen off is not power offWhen the screen dims and goes dark on its own, CAT keeps running: it still receives messages, relays traffic for other CATs and reads the sensors. The first tap or button press on a dark screen only wakes it and does nothing else.

Battery and charging#

  • The battery icon is at the top right of the home screen and the Dashboard. It animates while charging. It stays hidden until the battery gauge has reported once after boot.
  • Any USB-C charger works. A completely flat battery needs a proper charger (1.5 A or more) to start straight away; on a weak port CAT stays off until it has charged enough, which can take a while.
  • At 5% battery CAT saves everything automatically and briefly shows Critical battery Saving node.... If the cell gets too low, CAT saves and switches itself off, and the e-paper shows Battery empty.
  • The exact charge level, cell voltage and charger state are on Settings → DEVELOPER → INFO → POWER (see developer pages).
Charge at room temperatureThe charger's temperature sensor input is bypassed on current boards, so the charger cannot stop on its own if the battery gets too hot or cold. Charge CAT indoors and don't leave it charging in a hot car or in direct sun.

First boot: set your time zone#

The first time the home screen appears, CAT asks for your time zone. The clock and message times use it, and it is never sent over the network. The list works offline and knows daylight-saving rules up to the year 2100.

B goes back one step. Pressing B on the first page skips the prompt: the clock stays on UTC and CAT asks again at the next start. You can change the zone later in Settings → TIME & TIMEZONE.

The home screen#

  • Five apps in a 3×2 grid: Dashboard, Garden, Explore, Chat and Settings.
  • Move with the D-pad. ◀ and ▶ step one tile (hold to repeat); ▲ and ▼ jump a row.
  • Open an app by releasing A, or tap its tile. If you move the highlight while holding A, releasing it does not open anything.
  • Unread messages show as a number on the Chat tile (99+ above 99).
  • The clock reads Syncing... until the board controller has reported the time after boot.

How saving works (important)#

CAT keeps your messages, contacts, settings and game progress in fast memory while it runs, and writes them to permanent storage only at certain moments. There is no automatic save every few minutes. If CAT loses power suddenly, or is reset, anything since the last save is lost.

CAT saves when you:

  • put it to sleep or power it down (Settings → POWER);
  • leave the Settings menu after changing something;
  • choose Settings → DEVELOPER → CONTROLS → FORCE SAVE;
  • start Doom, or enter the USB bootloader for a firmware update;
  • reach 5% battery (automatic).

While a save runs, a Saving... box covers the screen. An ordinary save takes about 4 seconds; a busy node can take 10 to 16 seconds. Don't unplug or reset during it.

Good habitUse POWER → SLEEP instead of letting the battery run out, and use FORCE SAVE before plugging CAT into a computer for an update.

Sounds and lights#

CAT is mostly quiet. You hear the message chime (if you turn on NEW MESSAGE TONE), a soft ambient loop in Explore, Party music, and game audio. There are no button clicks. The RGB LEDs only light up from the Party menu.
